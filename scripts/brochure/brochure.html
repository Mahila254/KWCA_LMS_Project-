Years of conservancy wisdom. Now one click away.

The Kenya Wildlife Conservancies Association has brought more than a decade of hard-won field experience into one online learning platform, open to everyone who lives with, works for and believes in Kenya’s conservancies.

The story

Kenya’s conservancies were not built in classrooms. They were built under acacia trees, at community meetings and in the field, by people who learned by doing.

For years that knowledge lived in workshop notes, manuals and the memories of experienced managers: valuable, but hard to reach.

The Conservancies Learning Hub changes that. It consolidates years of KWCA’s work into short, practical courses that anyone can take on a phone or a laptop, at their own pace, wherever they are.
